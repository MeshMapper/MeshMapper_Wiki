# Support MeshMapper

MeshMapper is free to use and always will be. It runs on servers I pay for, and it keeps growing: more regions, more wardrivers, more data every week. If MeshMapper has been useful to you or your mesh, a tip on Ko-fi helps keep it running.

## Where it goes

- **Servers and hosting** for the website, the map tiles and the MQTT broker
- **Development time** for the app, the website and new features

## Other ways to help

Money is not the only way to help. These make just as much difference:

- **Wardrive.** Every drive fills in coverage for your local mesh. See [Getting Started](app_getting_started.md).
- **Set up an observer.** Observers are what turn pings green on the map. See [MQTT Setup](mqtt-main.md).
- **Bring your region on board.** See [Onboarding New Regions](onboarding.md).
- **Report bugs and ideas.** See [Report Bugs & Features](reportbugs.md).
- **Help others** in the MeshMapper Discord and spread the word in your local mesh group.

Thank you!
