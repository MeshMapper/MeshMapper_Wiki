# Wardriving App

MeshMapper is a community-driven wardriving app for MeshCore mesh network devices. It connects to your MeshCore radio over Bluetooth, TCP (network/Wi-Fi), or USB (Android), sends pings across the mesh, and adds what it hears to the [MeshMapper community map](https://meshmapper.net/). Your location goes only to the MeshMapper server; by default, pings on the air carry an anonymous token, not your coordinates.

The app is available for Android and iOS:

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/meshmapper/id6758073991) | [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.meshmapper.app)
